
 « *Je n'ai fait celle-ci plus longue 
que parce que je n'ai pas eu le loisir 
de la faire plus courte.* »
 — Blaise Pascal
*Les Provinciales* (1657)

 *I have made this longer than usual because I have 
 not had time to make it shorter.*


To write less, you must think more. 
To speak clearly, you must wrestle with complexity. 
To communicate well, you must pursue clarity over quantity.
