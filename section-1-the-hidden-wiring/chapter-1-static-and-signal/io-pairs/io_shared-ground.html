It makes sense—just not to them.

The explanation is solid. The reasoning is clear. The structure holds.

Nothing feels confusing from where it was built.

And yet, it doesn’t connect.

The audience follows a different path. They arrive at a different meaning. They respond in ways that don’t match what was intended.

No signal is lost.

But nothing aligns.

Where does understanding begin?

Shift where the message begins.

  • What does the audience already believe to be true?
  • What are they likely to assume before reading?

Now decide:

  • What must be established before anything else can be understood?

The message does not become clearer by saying more.

It becomes possible by starting where the audience already stands.
