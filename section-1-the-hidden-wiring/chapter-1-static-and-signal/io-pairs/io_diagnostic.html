Something is off—but not in one place.

The message exists. The words are clear enough. The audience is present. The exchange moves forward.

Still, it doesn’t hold.

Parts of it work. Parts of it resist. The issue doesn’t sit in a single sentence. It appears across the message—in how it begins, where it lives, what it assumes, how it’s received.

It doesn’t fail all at once.

It gives way in layers.

Where is it breaking?

Locate it before making changes.

  • Is the direction unclear?
  • Is the channel weakening it?
  • Is the experience misaligned?
  • Is the feedback missing or misleading?

Now commit:

Choose one layer.

Work there first.

The message doesn’t become perfect.

It becomes workable.
