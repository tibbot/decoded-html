# Business Communication Outline

## Section I: **The Hidden Wiring**  
*Foundations of Communication*

1. **Static and Signal**  
*Understanding Communication*  
   - Models: SMR, Shannon–Weaver, Schramm, Transactional  
   - Noise & barriers, feedback loops, context  

2. **The Seven Shadows**  
*Principles of Effective Business Communication*  
   - The 7 Cs (introduced through discovery)  
   - Tone, clarity, concision  
   - Active listening, impression management, trust  

3. **Mirror, Mirror**  
*Ethical & Audience-Centered Communication*  
   - Audience analysis and purpose  
   - Audience-centered ethics (common ground, fairness)  
   - Legal considerations, accessibility as ethical obligation  



## Section II: **Blueprints and Shortcuts**  
*The Writing Process & Core Tools*

4. **Laying the Tracks**  
*Planning and Structuring Messages*  
   - Three-step writing process (planning, drafting, completing)  
   - Purpose statements (inform, persuade, resolve, request)  
   - Direct vs. indirect approach  

5. **Channel Surfing**  
*Crafting Messages Across Channels*  
   - Digital, synchronous vs. asynchronous, traditional channels  
   - Noise and barriers revisited in channel selection  
   - Plain language principles, cross-channel consistency  

6. **White Space Isn't Empty**  
*Designing for Clarity and Impact*  
   - Grammar, mechanics, style guides  
   - Document design (headings, chunking, white space, Gestalt)  
   - Visual clarity: charts, infographics, data storytelling  



## Section III: **The Message Arcade**  
*Types of Business Messages*

7. **Good News Travels Fast**  
*Routine and Positive Messages*  
   - Neutral/informative, approvals, goodwill  
   - Reinforcing relationships and credibility  

8. **The Bad News Sandwich**  
*Negative Messages*  
   - Refusals, denials, corrective feedback  
   - Delivering bad news with tact and responsibility  

9. **The Gentle Push**  
*Persuasive Messages*  
   - Aristotle's rhetorical triangle  
   - Monroe's Motivated Sequence, Cialdini's principles  
   - Persuasion in proposals, marketing, workplace advocacy  



## Section IV: **Reports, Receipts, and Revelations**  
*Reports, Research, and Presentations*

10. **Paper Trails**  
*Reports and Proposals*  
   - Informational vs. analytical reports  
   - Progress and feasibility reports  
   - Proposals (internal vs. external)  

11. **Evidence, Please**  
*Research and Evidence*  
   - Collecting, analyzing, citing sources  
   - Formal research reports with executive summaries  
   - Data integrity and ethics  

12. **Lights, Mic, Action**  
*Oral and Multimedia Presentations*  
   - Speech organization and delivery  
   - Visual aids, slide design, accessibility  
   - Q&A management, group presentations  



## Section V: **Leveling Up**  
*Professional Applications*

13. **The Job Quest**  
*Employment Communication*  
   - Resumes, cover letters, job-specific emails  
   - STAR method for interviews  
   - Networking and elevator pitches  

14. **Table Manners for Meetings**  
*Professional Etiquette & Collaboration*  
   - Email and meeting etiquette  
   - Internal communication networks (upward/downward/lateral)  
   - Team collaboration and conflict resolution  

15. **The Long Game**  
*Leadership, Responsibility, and Lifelong Learning*  
   - Leadership communication and impression management  
   - CSR communication, intercultural ethics  
   - Reflection, self-assessment, portfolio development
